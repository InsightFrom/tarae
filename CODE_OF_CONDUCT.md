# Code of Conduct

Tarae follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Public or private intimidation
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of project discussion

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported by opening a private security advisory or by contacting the maintainers through the contact channel listed in the repository.
